MANBEM Support

MANBEM plays the audiobooks you own and answers your questions about them using only what you've heard so far, so nothing is ever spoiled.

Need help? Email manbem13@myyahoo.com. Tell us which device you're using (iPhone or Mac) and what happened, and include a screenshot if you can.

Adding audiobooks

  • iPhone: tap + in the Library and choose a file or folder, share an audiobook to MANBEM from the Files app or AirDrop, or drop files into On My iPhone › MANBEM in the Files app.
  • Mac: drag an audiobook or a folder onto the MANBEM window or its Dock icon, or choose File › Add Audiobook…
  • MANBEM plays M4B, M4A, and MP3 files that aren't copy-protected. Books bought from Audible or the Apple Book Store are copy-protected and can't be added.
  • On the Mac you can also add PDFs and EPUBs to read on screen.

Ask, Recap, and Cast

  • Ask questions about the book, get a "Previously On…" recap, and see who's who, all from the parts you've heard.
  • These need a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.
  • MANBEM first turns what you've heard into text on your device. Answers get better as more of the book is ready; you'll see the progress at the top of the screen.

Use your own AI

  • In Settings > AI & Voices, connect your own OpenAI, Claude, or Gemini key to power Ask, Recap, and Cast, even on devices without Apple Intelligence.
  • Connect an ElevenLabs, OpenAI, or Gemini key for lifelike Read Aloud voices (MANBEM Pro). Apple's built-in voices work without a key.
  • You pay the service directly for what you use. Keys stay in your device's Keychain.

Playback

  • Smart Rewind plays a few seconds earlier after a pause. Voice Boost makes narrators louder and clearer. Both are in Settings › Playback.
  • Sleep timer: tap the moon. On iPhone, shake to add 10 minutes.
  • CarPlay and widgets show the book you're listening to, with Play and Pause.

iPhone and Mac

Add the same book to both, and MANBEM keeps your place in step through iCloud. Turn this off in Settings › Sync. Only your place is synced, never the books.

MANBEM Pro

MANBEM Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Buy it once and keep it on your iPhone, with no subscription. It adds:

  • Cloud Voices: lifelike Read Aloud voices from your own ElevenLabs, OpenAI, or Gemini account.
  • iPhone and Mac Sync: your place in every book, kept in step through iCloud.
  • Export Narration: save a book read aloud as one audio file.

Already bought it? Open Settings in MANBEM and tap Restore Purchases. You can also leave a tip in Settings to support MANBEM. Tips say thanks; they don't unlock anything.
