Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Updated November 7, 2026

In short: MANBEM doesn't collect, sell, or share any of your information. Your books, your listening, your questions, and their answers stay on your own devices.

What stays on your device

  • Your books. Audiobooks, PDFs, and EPUBs you add are copied into MANBEM's own folder on your iPhone or Mac. They're never uploaded anywhere.
  • Transcripts. MANBEM turns the parts you've heard into text using Apple's speech recognition, which runs on your device.
  • Ask, Recap, and Cast. Answers, recaps, and character notes are written by Apple Intelligence on your device, unless you connect your own AI service (below). Your questions and the book's text are never sent to us.
  • Listening Stats and settings. Minutes listened, streaks, bookmarks, and your choices in Settings are kept on your device.

iCloud sync

If you use MANBEM on both an iPhone and a Mac, it can keep your place in each book in step through your own iCloud account: the book's title and length, where you are, how far you've heard, and the speed. The books themselves are never synced. This goes through Apple's iCloud, under Apple's Privacy Policy; we can't see it. You can turn it off in MANBEM's Settings > Sync.

AI services you connect

MANBEM works without any outside AI. If you choose to connect your own account with OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), or ElevenLabs by adding your API key in Settings > AI & Voices, MANBEM asks you first, then sends only what that feature needs, directly from your device to the service you chose:

  • Ask, Recap, and Cast: your question and the parts of the book you've already heard.
  • Read Aloud: the text being read aloud.

Your key is stored only in your device's Keychain and is sent only to that service. We never receive your key or anything sent with it. The service's own terms and privacy policy apply, and it bills you directly. Remove a key at any time in Settings > AI & Voices to stop.

Downloads from Apple

The first time MANBEM transcribes a book in a language, your device may download Apple's speech model for that language from Apple. Nothing about your books is sent in that download.

Purchases

MANBEM Pro and tips in the Tip Jar are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, card, or Apple Account. Apple tells us only how many tips were left, in total.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.

Children

MANBEM doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
